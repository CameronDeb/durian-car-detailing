How long does a full detail take?

Most full interior and exterior details take 2 to 5 hours, depending on the size of the car and how much work it needs. Polishing and ceramic coatings take longer. I'll give you a time estimate when you book.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Arizona?

Honestly, yes. The Tucson sun, monsoon dust and hard water spots are rough on paint. A ceramic coating adds a tough protective layer that blocks UV and makes the dust rinse right off.
