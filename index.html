What areas of Tucson do you serve?

We're based on Tucson's east side and proudly serve Tanque Verde, the Catalina Foothills, the Sabino Canyon area, East Tucson, Midtown, Oro Valley, Marana, Vail, Catalina, and the greater Tucson metro area.

How long does a full car detail take?

A typical full interior and exterior detail takes 2 to 5 hours depending on your vehicle's size and condition. Polish and ceramic coating services take longer — we'll give you a time estimate when you book.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Arizona?

Absolutely. The intense Tucson sun, monsoon dust, and hard water spots are brutal on paint. A ceramic coating adds a durable protective layer that resists UV damage and makes washing off desert dust much easier.
